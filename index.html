Location

Right in the middle of everything

Ardmore sits on I‑35, about 90 minutes from both Oklahoma City and Dallas–Fort Worth. Connections to I‑40 and I‑44 put any city in the nation within three days by truck.

  • I‑35 corridor between OKC and DFW
  • BNSF mainline rail
